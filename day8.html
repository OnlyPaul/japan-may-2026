04 · Where to eat

Lunch · primary
Ekibenya Matsuri (Tokyo Station)
200+ regional ekiben (train bento) from all over Japan, all in one shop. Eat in the Marunouchi exit area or take to Kitte rooftop.
~¥1,200–1,800 Walk-in Fastest

05 · Don't miss

Visual references.

Nakajima-no-Ochaya
Floating tea house
Crosses the small bridge to the wooden tea house in the middle of the tidal pond. Matcha + wagashi set is ~¥850. Sit by the window, watch the carp.
Zojoji + Tokyo Tower
The frame
Stand at Zojoji main gate (Sangedatsumon) on the temple's south side. The tower lines up directly behind the gate roof. Best 30 min after sunset when tower lights kick on.
Tokyo Station for Kitte Marunouchi side, not Yaesu
  1. JR Tokyo Station — exit via Marunouchi South Exit (not Yaesu)
  2. The red-brick station building is on your right; Kitte building straight ahead across the plaza
  3. Take the elevator inside Kitte to 6F Garden — free rooftop with the best Station view
  4. Ekibenya Matsuri is back inside the station on the Marunouchi concourse
Zojoji approach Where to stand for the frame
  1. Toei Mita Line or Asakusa Line to Onarimon or Shibakoen
  2. Walk to Sangedatsumon — the big main gate
  3. Cross the road south of the gate, look back — Tower aligns behind the gate roof
  4. Wait until ~18:30 when tower lights are on and sky is deep blue
06 · Tips for today

Worth remembering.

Itoya 12 floors. Don't try to see all of them — take the elevator to 12F, work down. Best floors: 8F (travel), 4F (handmade paper), B1F (writing instruments).
Ginza Six rooftop is free. Take the express elevator up; matcha cafe + garden + city view at no charge.
Zojoji light-up timing. Tower lights on at sunset + 30 min. Don't arrive before 18:00 — flat-light photos.
