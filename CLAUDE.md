# Rule
- If you want to ask, prioritize using AskUserQuestion tool.

# Food preferences

- Do not suggest ramen.
- Do not suggest okonomiyaki.
- Prioritize Japanese cuisine. Western, Indian, etc. only as a last-resort fallback.
- Avoid restaurants that have branches in Bangkok (e.g., Yakiniku Like, Tonkatsu Maisen, Tonkatsu Wako, Saboten, Katsukura, Bills, Genki Sushi). Prefer single-location institutions or Japan-only chains.

# Meal budget

- Per-person meal budget: ≤ THB 1,000 (~¥4,400) for every meal.
- Exemptions (no budget cap): Akatsuki (Wed lunch, Kamakura) and Kaiten Sushi Ginza Onodera Honten (Sat lunch, Omotesando).
- Prefer walk-in friendly restaurants — reservation lead time is short, assume advance bookings may not be obtainable.
