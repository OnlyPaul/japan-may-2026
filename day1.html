04 · Don't forget

Pre-flight checklist.

Documents Passport + IDP + license
  1. Passport (6+ months validity)
  2. International Driving Permit (1949 Geneva) — Nippon won't accept Thai license alone
  3. Thai driving license (must travel with the IDP)
  4. Hotel reservation printouts (just in case at immigration)
Money + Data Cash, IC card, SIM
  1. JPY cash for Akatsuki, Tonkatsu Marugo (cash-only)
  2. Suica/Pasmo via Apple Wallet or physical card on arrival
  3. Travel eSIM activated before takeoff
  4. Credit cards w/ no FX fee · backup Visa/Mastercard
For Fuji mornings Warm layer + umbrella
  1. Light puffer or fleece (Fuji AM ~7°C)
  2. Compact umbrella (May = 10 rainy days expected)
  3. Slip-on shoes for shrines + teamLab Planets (Day 7 you'll get wet feet)
  4. Camera + power bank + spare SD
