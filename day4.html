05 · Don't miss

Visual references.

Owakudani
Sulfur vents at 1,044m
The boardwalk loop is short (~15 min). Smell is intense. Buy eggs while walking up — easier to carry than down.
Heiwa-no-Torii
The torii in Lake Ashi
Line up to step onto the platform for the photo (rope-line in place). 5–15 min wait on weekdays. Be quick — there's a rhythm to the queue.
Owakudani parking Drive up, don't take the ropeway
  1. From Fujinomiya, take Route 1 → Hakone Skyline (¥500 toll)
  2. Owakudani has a small paid lot — ¥500 for 2 hr, usually has space on weekdays
  3. If full, the ropeway station lot at Sounzan also works; bus up to Owakudani
  4. Volcanic activity occasionally closes the area — check status before driving up
Nippon Ikebukuro West return Where to drop the car
  1. West Exit (Nishi-guchi) of Ikebukuro Station — not the East side
  2. Office is on the basement level of the Nippon parking structure
  3. Staff walks the car with you to check for new damage — sign off, return keys + ETC
  4. Walk 8 min south to Tokyu Stay; or use the West Exit underground passage to avoid road crossings
06 · Tips for today

Worth remembering.

Fuel up before Hakone Skyline. No gas stations on the toll road. Last station in Gotemba.
Tokyo entry buffer. Mondays around the Shuto Expressway can stack — leave Hakone at 15:30, not 16:00.
Car return inspection. Walk around with the Nippon staff and photograph any pre-existing scratches before signing. Insurance disputes can hit days later.
