Markets + Odaiba retro street.

Breakfast · primary
Tsukiji Outer Market — graze
Multiple small bites: tamagoyaki on a stick (Marutake), uni-bowl (Yamacho), grilled scallop, A5 wagyu stick. Pay each stall as you go.

05 · Don't miss

Visual references.

Tsukiji Outer Market
Eat-and-walk graze
Don't go shop-to-shop in order. Walk the whole thing once, mark 3–4 stalls, then graze in your preferred order with appetite calibrated.
Rainbow Bridge
Sunset walk, Shibaura side
Free pedestrian path opens 9–21. Start from Shibaura-futo side; ~30 min one-way. Best light 18:00–18:30 in May.
teamLab Planets entry Where to enter, what to wear
  1. Yurikamome to Shin-Toyosu (not Toyosu) — exit toward the venue
  2. Have QR ticket open on phone before queue
  3. You'll go barefoot — wear shorts or rollup pants for the knee-deep water room
  4. Lockers free; carry as little as possible. Phone in pocket = wet phone risk
Toyosu Market entry Find the viewing decks
  1. Yurikamome to Shijo-mae Station
  2. Skywalk leads directly into the market complex
  3. Tuna auction viewing deck = Building 7 (Wholesale), 3F gallery
  4. Senkyaku Banrai retail street + restaurants = same complex, separate entry
06 · Tips for today

Worth remembering.

teamLab time slot is fixed. Aim to be at the entrance 15 min before slot — there's a security/shoe-removal queue.
Don't graze Tsukiji on a full stomach. Skip hotel breakfast or do a tiny one. The whole point of the market is the small-bite cadence.
Rainbow Bridge wind. The pedestrian path is suspended; gusts can be 6+ m/s. Light jacket worth carrying even on warm days.
