Marugo or one of two walk-in alts.

Dinner · alt 1
Kanda Yabu Soba
Est. 1880 — one of Tokyo's "Big 3" historic soba shops. Hand-cut seiro soba + tempura.
~¥1,500–3,000 Walk-in 8 min walk from Akihabara
Order is sung out loud by the staff at the door — an Edo-era tradition still going. Single location only.

Unagi specialist since 1733. Una-don, una-juu sets. Tatami seating. One Yamanote stop back from Akihabara.

05 · Don't miss

Visual references.

Nezu Shrine
Vermillion torii corridor
Walk through the senbon-torii from the bottom up — fewer people in the frame as you climb. Azalea field below is the May highlight.
Yanaka Ginza
The shotengai you've seen in films
Walk it from the Yuyake Dandan stairs end. Try the mentaiko korokke from Niku-no-Suzuki — line moves in 5 min.
Nippori Station for Yanaka Right exit for the stairs
  1. JR Yamanote / Keisei Line to Nippori Station
  2. Take the West Exit (Nishi-guchi)
  3. Cross the road, walk 5 min north — Yuyake Dandan is on your left
  4. The stairs lead directly down to Yanaka Ginza
Akihabara for Marugo Don't get lost in the otaku side
  1. JR Akihabara Station, take the Showa-dori Exit (not Electric Town)
  2. Cross under the elevated tracks, head south on Showa-dori
  3. Marugo is a small storefront with a vertical sign — easy to walk past
  4. Cash-only — there's a Seven-Eleven ATM 50m back if you forgot
06 · Tips for today

Worth remembering.

Yanaka cat-watch. The neighborhood is famously cat-friendly. Look up — they hang on shop awnings and signage rather than at street level.
Time the soba "sing-out" at Kanda Yabu. Stand by the front podium for 30 seconds to hear the staff chant the order — it's the show.
Marugo has odd hours. Closed Sundays + irregular Mondays. Double-check before queueing.
