05 · Don't miss

Visual references.

Chureito Pagoda
The view that's on every postcard
Climb to the upper observation platform (above the pagoda, not at it). Cherry trees still partially in late-season green-pink in mid-May.
Shiraito Falls
"White threads of water"
200-step descent from the upper lot. The wide fan-shape is best photographed mid-morning to early afternoon when the sun is on the falls.
Chureito parking Where to park + which staircase
  1. Park at Arakurayama Sengen Park lower lot (¥500 cash)
  2. Cross the road, walk up the path past the shrine
  3. Skip the easy stair set — the 400-step direct climb is faster than the gentle slope
  4. The shot is from the upper observation deck, not at the pagoda itself
Lake Tanuki torii Finding the right angle
  1. Drive to Tanukiko campground / west shore parking
  2. Walk 5 min south along the lake path
  3. The torii is small — easy to miss. Look for a row of telephoto lenses on tripods
  4. Best with morning light; midday it's flat
06 · Tips for today

Worth remembering.

Chureito = early start. By 09:30 the staircase is single-file all the way up. Pre-dawn arrival is the move if you can manage it.
Yakisoba is "the local food." Don't try to do Fujinomiya without it — there's a Yakisoba Academy museum if you're curious.
Hotel Mifujien onsen. Tattoos restricted at most public baths. Check policy at check-in; some allow with cover-up stickers.
