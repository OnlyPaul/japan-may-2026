05 · Don't miss

Visual references.

Tokyo Station
Sunday morning, no people
The plaza in front of the red-brick facade is empty enough on a Sunday morning to get an unobstructed wide shot. The line for the photo spot is 0 before 10:00.
N'EX
Narita Express E259
Reserved-seat only. Buy from JR machine; passport required for foreigner round-trip discount tickets (if eligible). Trolley service on board.
Tokyu Stay → Tokyo Station Two routes from Ikebukuro
  1. JR Yamanote direct: ~16 min, ¥210, every 3 min. Easy with bags via elevators
  2. Marunouchi Line: ~17 min, ¥210, direct to Tokyo Station (Marunouchi side). Closer to Ekibenya Matsuri
  3. If bringing big bags: use station coin lockers, ~¥600 large size
N'EX boarding platform Underground at Tokyo Station
  1. Tokyo Station — follow signs for N'EX (Narita Express)
  2. Platform is underground, Platform 1-4 (Sobu line basement)
  3. Reserved seat — coach + seat number printed on ticket
  4. Arrive 10 min before — N'EX is known for on-time departure
06 · Tips for the road home

Worth remembering.

Last-minute souvenirs. Tokyo Station gransta and Narita T1 airside both have full Japanese-confectionery selections. Don't panic-shop in Ikebukuro.
Tax-free check. If you bought tax-free items, keep them in the carry-on with the receipt envelope unopened until past security.
BKK arrival 22:15. Pick up car from Car Hub same night, drive home — or stay at an airport hotel and pick up Monday morning. Decide before you fly out.
