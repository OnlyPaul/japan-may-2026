05 · Don't miss

Visual references.

Shibuya Crossing
From Hikarie 11F
The free Sky Lobby view from Hikarie 11F beats most paid observation decks for the Crossing itself. Best at dusk + light rain (umbrellas in frame).
Tocho (TMG Building)
Free 202m observation
North or South tower, free entry. Night projection-mapping show runs on the exterior facade — watch from the plaza outside, not from inside the deck.
Omotesando → Onodera Walking line, no detour
  1. Tokyo Metro to Omotesando Station (Chiyoda / Ginza / Hanzomon lines)
  2. Take Exit A2 for the Aoyama-dori side
  3. Walk 3 min east — Onodera Honten is in a polished building with a small black sign
  4. If you booked online, show QR; if not, ask staff about same-day kaiten counter availability
Tocho approach + viewing Free observation deck logistics
  1. Toei Oedo Line to Tochomae, or 10-min walk from JR Shinjuku West Exit
  2. Enter at ground floor — security check (bag scan)
  3. Free elevator to 45F North or South Observatory (alternate days)
  4. For projection mapping: watch from outside the building, on the plaza facing the south face; ~19:30 nightly show
06 · Tips for today

Worth remembering.

Onodera red-button. Have two devices open with the booking page if possible. Slots are typically all gone in <30 seconds.
Avoid Takeshita-dori past 12:00. If you didn't go early, skip it. Cat Street has the same atmosphere with 1/3 the people.
Golden Gai etiquette. If you go, peek at the doorway sign — some bars are members-only or have cover charges. Be respectful with photos; no flash inside.
