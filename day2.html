05 · Don't miss

Visual references.

Oshino Hakkai
Frame Fuji behind the thatched roofs
Stand near the largest pond (Wakuike). The reflection of Fuji works only when the wind is dead-calm — usually before 13:00.
Fuji Oishi Park
Lavender slope viewpoint
Park in the upper lot and walk down. The classic shot is from the middle terrace — kochia + lavender frame the lake.
Nippon NRT pickup Where to find the desk after immigration
  1. Exit immigration, head down to 1F Arrival Lobby
  2. Walk to Counter Group D (rental car shuttles)
  3. Take the Nippon Rent-a-Car shuttle — free, every 10 min
  4. 5-min ride to the office; full pickup ~30 min including paperwork
Driving heads-up Right-hand drive · what surprises you
  1. Left-side traffic — same as Thailand, easier transition than for EU/US drivers
  2. ETC card included in rental — toll gates open automatically
  3. Stop fully at all unmarked intersections; cameras everywhere
  4. Speed limits are LOW — 60 km/h is highway, 40 km/h is normal road

Fujikawaguchiko Resort Hotel161 243 885*8735.4947, 138.7761

These are Denso Japan Mapcodes — the numeric codes every Japanese rental-car nav unit accepts. Hit the MAPCODE button on the nav keypad and enter the digits exactly (space-separated, then the * + 2-digit checksum). At pickup, request the English-language nav — same fleet, language toggle only. Lat/lng column is the fallback for the rare unit that doesn't take Mapcode.

06 · Tips for today

Worth remembering.

Fight jet lag aggressively. Sunlight after landing, no nap before 16:00. Get to bed at the hotel by 22:00.
Photograph the parking spot. Lawson Kawaguchiko area has multiple identical convenience-store lots. Easy to forget where you parked.
Fuji shy. Even with a perfect forecast, the summit can disappear behind a thin cloud cap for 20–40 min at a time. Don't panic — wait it out.
