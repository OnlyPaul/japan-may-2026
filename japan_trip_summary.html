01 · Critical Status

Four things to keep in mind.

Fuji visibility

Excellent forecast.

May 16 & 17 mornings 0% clouds, 30+ km visibility. Your photo plan should pay off.

Meal budget

≤ THB 1,000 per person.

~¥4,400 cap at every meal. Exemptions: Akatsuki (Wed lunch) + Onodera Honten (Sat lunch). Everything else: walk-in, casual tier.

Tokyo weather

Mid-20s°C, rain likely.

~10 rainy days expected in the May window. Pack a compact umbrella. Fuji mornings drop to ~7°C — bring a warm layer.

Driving in Japan

IDP required.

International Driving Permit (1949 Geneva Convention) required — a Thai license alone won't work at Nippon pickup.
